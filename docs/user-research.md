# 🌾 Agro-Shield User Research & Market Validation

## Overview

As part of the development and validation of Agro-Shield, our team conducted field visits and interviews with farmers, agricultural businesses, government agricultural representatives, and potential consumers.

The purpose of this research was to understand the real challenges faced by people involved in agricultural production, distribution, storage, and purchasing, and to validate whether Agro-Shield addresses genuine market needs.

## Field Research Findings

### 1. Green Harvest Food Stuff Industry

**Location:** Benue State

Green Harvest Food Stuff Industry produces various agricultural products, including flour and groundnut oil.

During our discussion, they explained that they often have difficulty finding and directly connecting with farmers who have the produce they need.

Currently, they sometimes rely on radio announcements to inform farmers that they are looking to purchase specific agricultural products.

They also identified water availability as one of the challenges affecting their operations.

**Key challenges identified:**

- Difficulty connecting directly with farmers
- Dependence on radio announcements to find suppliers
- Difficulty accessing reliable agricultural produce suppliers
- Water-related operational challenges

**Potential Agro-Shield opportunity:**

Agro-Shield could provide a digital marketplace where agricultural businesses can post what produce they need and connect directly with farmers who can supply them.

---

### 2. Ewake Poultry Farm

Ewake Poultry Farm highlighted challenges related to customer reach, payment, and visibility.

The farm currently receives many customers from the surrounding area, but some customers do not make full payments.

The owner also explained that people outside the immediate area often have difficulty locating the farm.

He expressed strong interest in a platform similar to an e-commerce marketplace such as Jumia, where customers could:

- Find the farm
- View available products
- Place orders
- Make payments
- Arrange collection or delivery

The owner indicated that he would be interested in registering on Agro-Shield if these capabilities were available.

**Key challenges identified:**

- Limited visibility outside the local area
- Difficulty reaching new customers
- Incomplete payments from some customers
- Difficulty locating the farm

**Potential Agro-Shield opportunity:**

Agro-Shield can help agricultural businesses become more discoverable while providing a structured ordering and payment system.

---

### 3. Benue State Ministry of Agriculture

Our team also engaged with a representative of the Benue State Ministry of Agriculture in Otukpo.

The representative explained that the government provides farmers with agricultural resources such as improved seeds, fertilizers, and other quality farming inputs.

However, one of their major challenges is reaching genuine farmers in different communities.

They are also interested in conducting seminars and agricultural education programmes across communities but face challenges with reaching and communicating with farmers effectively.

The representative expressed interest in partnering with Agro-Shield as a potential digital channel for reaching farmers and communicating agricultural opportunities, programmes, and educational information.

**Key challenges identified:**

- Difficulty identifying and reaching genuine farmers
- Difficulty communicating government agricultural programmes
- Challenges organizing outreach across different communities
- Need for better channels for agricultural education and seminars

**Potential Agro-Shield opportunity:**

Agro-Shield could provide a digital communication and outreach channel through which government agricultural agencies can connect with verified farmers and share:

- Agricultural programmes
- Improved seeds
- Fertilizers
- Farming information
- Training opportunities
- Community seminars

### 4. Smallholder Farmers

Our team also interviewed three farmers.

A major issue raised was **post-harvest loss**.

The farmers explained that after harvesting, they often struggle to find legitimate buyers willing to purchase their produce at a fair price.

They also reported that some market buyers attempt to purchase produce at very low prices.

Farmers are placed in a difficult situation:

Harvest
   ↓
Find Buyer
   ↓
Buyer Offers Low Price
   ↓
Farmer Must Choose:
   ├── Accept Low Price
   └── Refuse and Risk Produce Spoiling

   The farmers expressed interest in a platform where they could advertise their produce before or around harvest time so that legitimate buyers could see the expected produce and place orders in advance.

**Key challenges identified:**

Post-harvest losses
Difficulty finding legitimate buyers
Low prices offered by some market buyers
Lack of direct access to larger markets
Risk of produce spoiling while waiting for buyers

### Potential Agro-Shield opportunity:

Agro-Shield can enable farmers to list upcoming or available produce so that verified buyers can discover the produce and potentially place orders before or around harvest.

This could help farmers reduce the time between harvesting and selling.

### 5. Potential Consumers

We also spoke with members of the public who are potential consumers of agricultural products.

They expressed interest in having a platform where they could order food and agricultural products without physically visiting different markets to search for what they need.

**Key challenges identified:**

Time spent searching for food products
Difficulty finding specific agricultural products
Need to physically visit markets
Lack of convenient digital ordering options

### Potential Agro-Shield opportunity:

Agro-Shield could provide consumers with an online marketplace where they can discover agricultural products, place orders, and eventually arrange payment and delivery.
